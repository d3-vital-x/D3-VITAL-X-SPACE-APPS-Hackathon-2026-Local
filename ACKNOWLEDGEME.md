## 📱 Mobile-Edge Research Development

A distinctive aspect of the D³ VITAL-X research workflow is its development under resource-constrained mobile computing conditions.

* **Mobile-Edge Computation Notice:** Heavy computational models, mathematical derivations, software architecture, and data-analysis pipelines were structured, evaluated, and executed using a resource-constrained Redmi 9 mobile hardware environment, together with cloud/notebook resources where appropriate.

*This constraint motivated a lightweight, modular, reproducible architecture designed to separate public interfaces from computationally intensive research components.*

## 🔐 Data Integrity, Provenance & Responsible Research

The project follows strict research-engineering principles:

* Preserve raw inputs whenever possible.
* Calculate integrity hashes for uploaded data.
* Maintain provenance metadata.
* Distinguish measured results (C1) from computational observations (C2) and hypotheses (C3).
* Require human review for research-oriented anomaly interpretation.
* Keep proprietary research-core implementation separate from public interfaces.
